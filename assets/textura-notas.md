# Textura de la aurora
Archivo: aurora-textura.png. Creada con la herramienta integrada ImageGen (no CLI), usando como referencia la imagen proporcionada por el usuario.

Prompt utilizado:
Edit the supplied reference into a straight, horizontal aurora TEXTURE asset for a real-time audio-reactive ribbon. Preserve the reference's exact distinctive soft dusty airbrushed grain, azure/deep blue body, mint green regions, pastel lilac purple diffuse edges, and irregular mixing of colors. IMPORTANT: straighten the wavy ribbon into a horizontal straight band; we will bend it in code afterward. The ribbon runs fully from left edge to right edge, centered vertically, occupying about 55% of canvas height. Wide 1536x512 landscape. Mix green and blue in broad irregular clouds that drift across the band, with lilac spreading around its edges. Avoid uniform parallel stripes, bright neon glowing tubes, hard edges, stars, text, arrows, scenery or checkerboard. The reference checkerboard is baked into its pixels: remove it completely and use a genuinely transparent alpha background. The band must have softly feathered semi-transparent edges fading to fully transparent top and bottom. Make the cloud body dense enough to keep original blue and green on both light and dark backgrounds, finely granular but soft. Only the horizontal texture, no UI, no grid.
